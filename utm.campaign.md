# UTM Campaign — `utm.campaign`

**Ordenação padrão:** `id`

---

## Campos Obrigatórios

- `name` **(char)** — Campaign Identifier ⚠️ obrigatório
- `title` **(char)** — Campaign Name ⚠️ obrigatório
- `user_id` **(many2one)** — Responsible ⚠️ obrigatório → `res.users`
- `stage_id` **(many2one)** — Stage ⚠️ obrigatório → `utm.stage`

## Campos Principais

- `id` **(integer)** — ID 🔒 readonly
- `active` **(boolean)** — Active
- `is_auto_campaign` **(boolean)** — Automatically Generated Campaign
  > Allows us to filter relevant Campaigns
- `color` **(integer)** — Color Index
- `create_date` **(datetime)** — Created on 🔒 readonly
- `write_date` **(datetime)** — Last Updated on 🔒 readonly
- `ab_testing_completed` **(boolean)** — A/B Testing Campaign Finished 🔒 readonly
- `ab_testing_schedule_datetime` **(datetime)** — Send Final On
  > Date that will be used to know when to determine and send the winner mailing
- `ab_testing_winner_selection` **(selection)** — Winner Selection
  > Selection to determine the winner mailing that will be sent.
  > Opções: `manual` (Manual), `opened_ratio` (Highest Open Rate), `clicks_ratio` (Highest Click Rate), `replied_ratio` (Highest Reply Rate), `crm_lead_count` (Leads), `sale_quotation_count` (Quotations), `sale_invoiced_amount` (Revenues)

## Relacionamentos

- `tag_ids` **(many2many)** — Tags → `utm.tag`
- `mailing_mail_ids` **(one2many)** — Mass Mailings → `mailing.mailing`
- `ab_testing_winner_mailing_id` **(many2one)** — A/B Campaign Winner Mailing → `mailing.mailing`
- `company_id` **(many2one)** — Company → `res.company`
- `currency_id` **(many2one)** — Currency 🔒 readonly → `res.currency`

## Campos Calculados (readonly)

- `click_count` **(integer)** — Number of clicks generated by the campaign 🔒 readonly
- `use_leads` **(boolean)** — Use Leads 🔒 readonly
- `crm_lead_count` **(integer)** — Leads/Opportunities count 🔒 readonly
- `mailing_mail_count` **(integer)** — Number of Mass Mailing 🔒 readonly
- `is_mailing_campaign_activated` **(boolean)** — Is Mailing Campaign Activated 🔒 readonly
- `ab_testing_mailings_count` **(integer)** — A/B Test Mailings # 🔒 readonly
- `received_ratio` **(float)** — Received Ratio 🔒 readonly
- `opened_ratio` **(float)** — Opened Ratio 🔒 readonly
- `replied_ratio` **(float)** — Replied Ratio 🔒 readonly
- `bounced_ratio` **(float)** — Bounced Ratio 🔒 readonly
- `quotation_count` **(integer)** — Quotation Count 🔒 readonly
- `invoiced_amount` **(integer)** — Revenues generated by the campaign 🔒 readonly
